Tulum Boho Living Room: Sheer Linen Curtains, Gold Leaf Art and Live-Edge Table

A Tulum boho living room with sheer linen curtains, a rustic timber beam ceiling, a white curved sofa, a live-edge wooden coffee table and a framed gold leaf artwork.

Long sheer curtains let the afternoon light in and move with the breeze. Above, a ceiling of rustic timber beams gives the room its Yucatan character.

A live-edge coffee table, dried grasses in a clay vase and a framed gold leaf artwork complete the corner. Keep the sofa white and let the wood and light do the work.
