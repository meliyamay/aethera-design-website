Boho Living Room: Curved Chukum Sofa, Blush Cushions and Gold Leaf Art

A boho living room with a curved white chukum sofa, blush pink cushions, a round wooden coffee table, a shaggy Berber rug and a gold leaf painting, with palm trees outside the window.

The sofa is built into the room: a soft, curved bench of white chukum plaster that follows the wall. Blush pink and sand cushions make it feel warm, and the window brings in the palms.

A round wooden table, woven poufs and a shaggy rug with simple lines keep the room low and calm. One piece of gold leaf art on the wall is all the shine it needs.
