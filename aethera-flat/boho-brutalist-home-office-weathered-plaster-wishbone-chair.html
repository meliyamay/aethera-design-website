Boho Brutalist Home Office: Weathered Plaster, Linen Drape and Wishbone Chair

A home office nook with a distressed lime plaster wall, a rustic wood desk, a Wishbone chair, an oversized linen curtain and an oval jute rug.

A desk does not have to look like an office. Weathered plaster, a worn wood table and one classic chair make a place you actually want to sit down.

Keep the setup light: a laptop, a notebook, a cup. The woven disc on the wall and the soft curtain do the decorating.
