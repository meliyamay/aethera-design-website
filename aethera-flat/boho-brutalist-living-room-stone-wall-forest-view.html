Boho Brutalist Living Room: Stone Wall, Rattan Pendant and Forest View

A living room with a sculpted rock wall, floor-to-ceiling glass facing the forest, an oversized rattan pendant, a low stone coffee table and deep linen seating.

The room works because everything sits low and heavy: the stone table, the floor cushions, the sofa. Above it, one big rattan pendant adds air and light.

Add dried pampas in a clay vase and a few candles on the table. Then let the forest outside be the artwork.
