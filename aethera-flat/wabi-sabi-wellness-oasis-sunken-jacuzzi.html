Wabi-Sabi Wellness Oasis: Sunken Jacuzzi with Forest View

A Boho Brutalist bathroom where a raw concrete ceiling meets soft draped linen, with a circular stone jacuzzi facing a floor-to-ceiling forest window.

A bath becomes a retreat when the view does half the work. A circular stone tub, a woven pendant and a low table with candles are all this room needs.

Soften raw concrete with long linen drapes and keep the lighting warm and low. The forest outside does the rest.
