Boho Brutalist Kitchen: Plaster Hood, Concrete Island and Open Oak Shelves

A kitchen with a sculpted plaster range hood, a raw concrete island with a travertine feel, open oak shelves and woven baskets.

The kitchen stays calm because the big shapes are simple: a curved hood, a solid island, shelves that carry the story. Handmade bowls and baskets add the warmth.

Choose one hero material, here plaster, and repeat it softly. Then let wood, rattan and clay do the rest.
