Aethera Design is a collection of Boho Brutalist interiors, Wabi-Sabi spaces and art, inspired by life in Tulum and the Yucatan.

Chukum plaster, concrete, stone, linen, rattan and candlelight: natural materials and imperfect surfaces that make a room calm and grounded.

Here I collect the spaces, objects and ideas I keep coming back to, from interior design and art to spaces for healing and wellness.

You will find interior design, art, spaces made for rest, and holistic rituals that make a home feel like a retreat.
