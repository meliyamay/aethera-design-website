Minimalist Boho Bedroom: Board-Formed Concrete, Linen Bedding and Jute Rug

A moody minimalist bedroom with board-formed concrete walls and ceiling, rumpled undyed linen on a low wood platform bed and a glowing clay lamp.

Concrete can feel cold, until it meets crumpled linen and a single warm light. Here the clay lamp is the only accent, and it is enough.

Stay in warm greige, avoid pure white, and let the bedding look slept in. The room gets softer the less perfect it is.
