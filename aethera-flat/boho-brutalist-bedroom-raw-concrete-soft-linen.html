Boho Brutalist Bedroom: Raw Concrete Wall and Soft Linen Curtains

A bedroom where a raw concrete wall meets billowing linen curtains, a low reclaimed wood platform bed and a warm glow underneath.

Rough and soft work best when they share a room. The concrete wall brings weight and calm, while undyed linen, a chunky jute rug and a small clay lamp keep the space gentle.

To get the look, keep the palette to stone, sand and bone. Let the bed sit low, hide a warm light under the platform, and choose bedding that is allowed to crumple.
